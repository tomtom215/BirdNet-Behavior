Six surfaces, one at a time

Production scatters analytics across six places (/heatmap, /migration, /analytics/dawn-chorus, /correlation, /timeseries, /analytics), three of them top-level nav tabs. Here they're one Patterns home with a tab per question — so a hobbyist never meets six analytical surfaces at once, and a researcher still reaches every one in a click.

Grounded in real source: the circadian polar (viz::circadian_polar), the co-occurrence chord + matrix (viz::chord_diagram / cooccurrence_matrix), the heatmap grid + heat ramp (heatmap.rs::render_heatmap_svg / heat_color), the migration ridgeline (viz::ridgeline) and the hourly bars are ported faithfully and render against the real app.css. Every tab opens with one picture, states the plain-English meaning, and tucks the table behind a "see the numbers" disclosure — so the funnel/retention jargon never lands on Pat unexplained.
