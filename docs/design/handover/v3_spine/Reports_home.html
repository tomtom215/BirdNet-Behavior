The recap, in one place

Production scatters the look-back across /weekly, /year-in-review and /history. Here they're one Reports home: Weekly (the Sunday recap a hobbyist looks forward to), Year in review (the 52-week tape, leaderboard & milestones), and History (a calendar of past days you can open to that day's detail). Both editorial pages are @media print-ready — Cmd+P drops a clean PDF the day the weekly lands.

Grounded in real source: the weekly & year reports mirror weekly_report.rs / year_in_review.rs (top species, new discoveries, 7-day & 52-week charts, milestones), and History mirrors history.rs (date browser with per-day hourly bars). Heat ramp, sparkline, hourly bars and species thumbs are the ported real renderers, against the real app.css.
