Northwood Station

Everything that keeps your station running — health first, then the settings, in plain groups.

One toolbox, six plain groups

Production splits station management across the read-only /system page and twelve flat /admin/* tabs (Overview · Settings · Audio · Species · Migration · Rules · Quality · Notifications · Accounts · Backups · System · Diagnostics). For a non-technical operator that's a wall. Here it's one Station home — the heir to BirdNET-Pi's "Tools/Settings" — grouped by task: Health (is it working?), Capture (what am I recording?), Alerts (tell me when…), Data (keep it safe / bring it in), Settings (my preferences), Access (who can get in).

Health leads — the audit's "single most important missing screen" for a multi-stream field deployment. The per-source panel, vitals (df-correct disk math), last-detection freshness (the deadman watchdog), queued-uploads (the store-and-forward queue), and the doctor checklist all surface data the supervisor and metrics already emit. Everything renders against the real app.css.
