Production splits audio across /recordings (the clip browser) and /listen (live per-source audio + spectrogram). Here they're one Recordings home: Clips (browse, play, lock & bulk-review captured detections, each with its spectrogram) and Live (the real-time scrolling sonogram with a source picker and a live detection trickle).

Grounded in real source: clips mirror recordings.rs (HTTP-Range streaming so scrubbing seeks without a full re-download, lock-from-purge, the shared illustrated empty state), and Live mirrors listen.rs / the spectrogram WebSocket producer.
