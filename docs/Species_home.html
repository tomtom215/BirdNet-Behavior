One home, three ways to look

Production splits this across three top-level destinations — /species (list), /gallery (photos) and /life-list (growth curve). They answer the same question, so here they're one Species home with a view switcher: List (counts & trends), Photos (the gallery), Life list (every species ever, with the accumulation curve — the single most emotionally resonant chart for a hobbyist). Tap any bird for its detail page.

Grounded in real source: the list rows mirror species_list_partial (rank · avatar · 14-day sparkline · count · confidence), the detail page mirrors species_detail.html (residency pills, cross-links to heatmap/co-occurrence, circadian, trend, companions), the life-list curve is viz::accumulation_curve, and photos use the bnb-photo drop-slot. All against the real app.css.
