## What the integration changed

- `295cf5a`: two lanes each brought a Wilson interval. `birdnet-db`'s copy is removed and the reviewer-verdict
  panel calls `birdnet_behavioral::interval::wilson`; the four values its test held are now a test of that
  function. The same commit makes the reporting-frequency figure say that its listened days are neighbours in
  the calendar and not independent trials, so its interval is the narrowest the uncertainty can be (Method
  panel, `reporting.rs`, honesty rule 10). An interval that allows for it is N25-02.
- `a098719`: `species-colours.mjs` is a step in `a11y.yml`; four doc sentences corrected.
- N25-01 was found by asking what RES-10's default does to a full disk: with every remaining clip protected,
  the purge removed nothing and no condition was raised. The claim was established by reading and then by
  three tests seen red.
- `8e43188` and `ca644e1`: the generated CLI help, and a query struct the slim build never read.
  Both were preflight failures on the integrated tree.

## Behaviour a station owner will notice

- Reviewed and commented audio is kept by the age limit, the per-species cap and the disk-full purge unless
  `BIRDNET_RETAIN_REVIEWED_AUDIO=false`. A station with a cap will keep more audio than before.
- Chart colours change once: a species now has one colour everywhere, and species outside the station's eight
  most-heard are drawn in the neutral.
- `/api/v2/analytics/retention`: `rate` may be `null`, and each entry has `eligible`.
- Schema 66.
