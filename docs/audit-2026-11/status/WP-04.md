## What was not verified

- Nothing ran on a Raspberry Pi, against a real stereo microphone or camera, or under systemd. That ffmpeg's
  `pan=mono|c0=c1` works on a real stereo RTSP stream is reasoning from ffmpeg's documentation, not a capture. A
  `Right` pick on a mono camera is expected to make ffmpeg refuse the filter at start-up (CONJECTURED, not tried).
- Whether two microphones' segments really settle in one sweep in the field (MSC-01's conjecture) was not
  measured; the gate shows the old split is unfair whenever they do.
- The CodeQL, image, install and upgrade smoke tests and the aarch64 cross-check run only in CI. The other browser
  gates (`layout.mjs`, `qa.mjs`, `legibility.mjs` and the rest of `a11y.yml`) were not run.
