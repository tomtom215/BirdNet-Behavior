## Verified, and how

- Every gate failed against the old behaviour before its fix (assertion text in each commit message). MSC-17's two surviving mutants, found by CI's mutation job on this PR (run 45311787760), were applied by hand and each failed the new test.
- On the integrated tree, with `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0`: `cargo check --workspace --all-targets --all-features` exit 0; `cargo test -p birdnet-db` no failures; `daemon::processor::` 74 passed; `a_detection_keeps_every_comment` 9 passed; the MSC-01/MSC-17 birdnet-core gates 15 passed. That run was before MSC-10 to MSC-13 were picked; `scripts/preflight.sh --tests` on the final commit is reported on the pull request.
- The MSC-20 card was rendered with `app.css` at 1280 and 390 px; the Capture forms with the new channels field were rendered by the delegated session at both widths on the screenshot fixture, and the edit form at 390 px was looked at here.
- The pan filter was run through the installed ffmpeg (6.1.1): a bare `pan=mono|c0=c1` on a mono input gives −91 dB (silence), which is why `aformat=channel_layouts=stereo` precedes it.

## Not verified

- Nothing on real hardware: ALSA `CARD=` names against a real `/proc/asound/cards`, the pan filter against a real PipeWire device or camera, shedding under a real backlog.
- The browser gates (axe, qa, layout) on the changed Capture and detection pages, and the comments page's new `.dc-shared` line in a browser.
- A downgrade past migration 61: a SQLite probe showed an older binary's quarantine insert is refused ("ON CONFLICT clause does not match"), so a downgraded station quarantines nothing; this is stated in the migration and the CHANGELOG, not tested end to end.
