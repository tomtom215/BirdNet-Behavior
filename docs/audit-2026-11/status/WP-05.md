## Decisions taken in this package, and why

- **Effort is analysed audio, and the deadman still measures uptime.** Once `recording_effort` holds analysed
  seconds, a station whose classifier analyses nothing would never accrue "time listened" and the deadman, which
  exists for that station, would never fire. `total_recording_seconds` now counts uptime throughout (legacy rows,
  less what the hourly table repeats, plus hourly `seconds_up`), with its own gate.
- **The rate divides by the sum of sources' listening.** The analytics copy has no `Source` (MSC-06, WP-06), so a
  per-source rate is not possible yet; the sum gives detections per source-hour, which equals the one-source rate
  for co-located sources and the mean per-source rate otherwise.
- **The trend floor is relative.** The first version judged a day against half of 24 hours; a station on a
  recording schedule shorter than 12 hours would have had every day judged unlistened. `6d4d55c` judges against
  half the station's median day with any audio, with a gate (`a_scheduled_station_is_judged_against_its_own_day`)
  that failed on the 24-hour floor.
