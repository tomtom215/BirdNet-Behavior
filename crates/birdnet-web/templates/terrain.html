Soundscape terrain

The station's sound, as a landscape

Across: the hours of the day. Down: the days, the newest at the bottom. The colour and, in 3D, the height of each patch are how much was heard in that hour of that day. A ridge that runs the length of the picture is an hour that is busy every day; a single peak is a day something happened.

Point at the picture, or use the arrow keys, to read an hour.

From none to — detections in the busiest hour. Colour and height are the square root of the count, so a quiet hour beside a busy one is still there.
Flat and hatched: the station was not listening that day, or the hour has not happened yet. That is not the same as quiet.

Grid: arrow keys move through the hours and days. 3D terrain: drag to turn it, scroll (after clicking it) or pinch to zoom; arrow keys turn it, plus and minus zoom, Home puts it back, and Shift with the arrows moves the cursor. Everything is also in the table below.
